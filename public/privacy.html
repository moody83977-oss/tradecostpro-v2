Privacy Policy for TradeCost Pro

Last updated: October 2026

TradeCost Pro ("we", "our", or "us") provides a job estimation, invoicing, and contractor utility application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data processed through our application.

1. Information We Collect

TradeCost Pro is designed to prioritize offline storage and on-device privacy:

  • Estimates and Job Data: Customer names, addresses, line items, and job costs are stored locally on your device storage.
  • Voice Input: When you use Voice-to-Estimate, voice transcripts are processed solely for parsing job details and are not stored or sold to third parties.
  • Payment Details: For GCash or Stripe payments, transaction references are stored on your device to keep track of paid invoices. We do not store banking passwords or credit card PINs.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Generate accurate contractor quotes, invoices, and material calculations.
  • Provide local backup and quick PDF invoice exports for your clients.
  • Facilitate direct customer payments via GCash and Stripe.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your client list and pricing settings remain securely stored on your device using browser local storage and offline cache. We do not share or sell your business financials to any external advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may integrate with standard industry payment processors (GCash, Stripe) when you initiate transactions. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.
